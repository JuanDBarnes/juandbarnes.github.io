I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Economics at [Universidad de San Andrés](https://www.udesa.edu.ar/).
I have completed the coursework for this degree, and I am now working on my thesis under the guidance
of Facundo Albornoz (Nottingham–UdeSA). In addition, I serve as a Consultant (Research Assistant) at the
Socioeconomic Research Department at [CAF—Development Bank of Latin America and the Caribbean](https://www.caf.com/).

My research interests are in labor economics, development, and empirical IO. My current research
focuses on firm data from Peru to examine how the adoption of the internet and the enforcement of
labor regulations influence productivity and employment composition. Alongside this firm-level work,
I study rural labor markets in Latin America with Dolores de la Mata (CAF), Mariana Marchionni, and
Iván Albina (CEDLAS-UNLP), examining why workers who move out of agriculture often remain in
vulnerable employment.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Economics from [Universidad Nacional del Nordeste](https://www.unne.edu.ar/).
I previously worked in public sector consulting, mainly focused on regional and development policy,
at PUNTO Labs and at the Provincial Institute of Statistics of Chaco.

In November 2026, I will attend the [RIDGE Summer School](https://www.ridge.org.uy/) and the
LACEA-LAMES Annual Meeting in Lima.
