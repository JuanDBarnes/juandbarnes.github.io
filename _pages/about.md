I am a Master's candidate in Economics at [Universidad de San Andrés](https://www.udesa.edu.ar/),
where I have completed the coursework and am currently writing my thesis, and a Research Assistant
at [CAF – Development Bank of Latin America and the Caribbean](https://www.caf.com/).

My research interests are in **labor economics**, **applied microeconomics** and **firm dynamics**.
At CAF I work on the flagship Economic and Development Report, studying demographic transitions and
labor markets across Latin America using household survey microdata. My thesis, advised by Facundo
Albornoz, examines how internet use relates to plant-level productivity in Peru.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Economics from [Universidad Nacional del Nordeste](https://www.unne.edu.ar/).
I previously worked on energy transition and regional development policy at PUNTO Labs, and as a data
analyst at the Provincial Institute of Statistics of Chaco.

In November 2026 I will attend the [RIDGE Summer School](https://www.ridge.org.uy/) and the
LACEA-LAMES Annual Meeting in Lima.
