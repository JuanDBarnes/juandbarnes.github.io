With Dolores de la Mata (CAF), Mariana Marchionni and Iván Albina (CEDLAS-UNLP).

Published as a CAF Working Paper in September 2026. Currently under review.

The paper examines the persistence of economic vulnerability in rural Latin America and the enduring
rural–urban gap against the backdrop of substantial changes in rural households and labor markets over
the first quarter of the twenty-first century.
